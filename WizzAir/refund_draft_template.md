# Wizz Air — Refund Application Draft (Delay or Cancellation)

Sources (official):
- Cancelled flight: https://www.wizzair.com/en-gb/help-centre/delay-cancellation-and-refund/cancelled-flight/cancelled-flight-what-to-do
- Delayed flight (5h+ / overnight): https://www.wizzair.com/en-gb/help-centre/delay-cancellation-and-refund/delayed-and-rescheduled-flight/delayed-flight/over-five-hours-and-overnight-delays
- Refund processing time: https://www.wizzair.com/en-gb/help-centre/delay-cancellation-and-refund/refund/refund-processing-time-and-status

Key rules to respect:
- Choice of remedy available **up to 3 hours before the original departure time** (cancellation page).
- Remedies offered by Wizz: (1) accept alternative flight, (2) free rebook within 14 days before / 30 days after original date, (3) full refund as WIZZ credits, (4) refund to **original payment method**.
- WIZZ credits → cash conversion goes through the credits-to-cash form linked from the refund pages.
- Refunds in WIZZ credits: within **48 h**. Claim-form refunds: processed within **30 days**; card/bank transfer may take up to 30 days to arrive.
- Booking made via an agency/OTA: the agency must handle it (or give you: 6-digit confirmation code, email, phone, last 4 digits + expiry of the payment card).
- EC261 compensation (arrival ≥ 3 h late) is a **separate claim** from the refund — see the EC261 claim section below.

## Submission checklist

- [ ] Confirm who cancelled / actual delay hours before choosing the draft.
- [ ] Direct booking → WIZZ Account / Live Chat / call centre / cancellation-email link.
- [ ] Agency booking → send to the agency; Wizz Air will not modify it without the 6-digit code, email, phone, last 4 digits + expiry of the card.
- [ ] Make the remedy selection before the 3-hour pre-departure deadline when possible.
- [ ] Do not submit duplicate claims — track via "Your Claim" in the WIZZ Account.
- [ ] EC261 compensation is not owed when extraordinary circumstances caused the delay; expect Wizz to invoke it.
